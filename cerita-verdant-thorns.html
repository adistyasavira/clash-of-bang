Story Of

The Verdant Thorns

Commander: Xylia

Backstory:

A scouting force that is at one with nature. Xylia trained them to be able to hide behind a single leaf. They often appear suddenly from within the earth or out of trees.

Specialization:

Guerrilla Warfare. Experts in natural traps and ambushes within forests or difficult terrain.

Xylia

Backstory:

A legendary female knight who fell in a great battle centuries ago. Her wrathful spirit could not find peace until Malakor found her and offered a deal: a new spectral body and a chance to fight again. Now, she leads the clan's aerial legion, bringing terror from the skies.
