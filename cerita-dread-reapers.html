Story Of

The Dread Reapers

Commander: Valthazar

Backstory:

This troop consists of knights who have lost all sense of fear. Valthazar trained them in the Valley of Death so they could manipulate the enemy's fear. They wear tattered black robes over armor that exudes a chilling aura.

Specialization:

Psychological Warfare & Sabotage. Before attacking, they unleash a black mist that causes enemies to hallucinate.

Valthazar

Backstory:

Once a just human king in the region now known as Sanur, his ambition to protect his people from destruction led him to strike a bargain with an ancient power in the depths of the ocean trenches. That power protected him but demanded a price: his soul. Now, he rules from an obsidian throne, the physical embodiment of the Void's will cold and unstoppable.
