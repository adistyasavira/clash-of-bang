Story Of

The Silver Sentinels

Commander: Lyra

Backstory:

A unit of archers who move with immense grace. Lyra trained them to breathe in rhythm with the wind. Legend has it that their arrows never miss, guided by Lyra's magical melody.

Specialization:

Long-Range Precision. They can rain down thousands of arrows upon the enemy from distances far beyond the reach of the naked eye.

Lyra

Backstory:

Lyra grew up in the mystical forests surrounding Bandung, where shadows possess a life of their own. From a young age, she was trained by the Order of the Moonless Night to become an invisible hunter. She does not use physical arrows; instead, she condenses shadow energy into lethal projectiles. Lyra is the clan's fastest spy and assassin.
