About Clash of BaNG

What started as a small online forum in 2018 has grown into a vibrant community of fantasy gaming enthusiasts. Clash of BaNG brings together players from across Indonesia, with regional hubs in Jakarta, Bandung, and Sanur.

We specialize in strategy guides, troop analysis, hero tutorials, and fan-created content. Our community's favorite focus? The legendary Dark Troops, chosen by our founders as the ultimate force in fantasy warfare.

Whether you're a beginner seeking guidance or a veteran looking to share your expertise, Clash of BaNG welcomes all players to join our growing family.
