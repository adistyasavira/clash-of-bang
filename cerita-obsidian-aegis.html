Story Of

The Obsidian Aegis

Commander: Obsidion

Backstory:

An elite unit comprised of giant, large-bodied soldiers. They wear armor forged from volcanic rock, tempered directly by Obsidion. They are the "Not An Inch Backward" unit.

Specialization:

Absolute Defense. They form an impenetrable Testudo (tortoise) formation that cannot be breached even by cannon fire.

Obsidion

Backstory:

Obsidion is not a living creature, but an ancient guardian statue reanimated by Malakor using Void magic. His body is formed from the hardest volcanic rock beneath Bandung, powered by an infinite core of shadow energy. He is a walking fortress, completely loyal to the clan leader's commands.
